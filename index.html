Help & Unity rules

Matching

Side neighbours fit when one socket equals the other reversed (io ↔ oi, f ↔ f). A tile fits on top of another when its negY equals the lower tile's posY exactly.

Unity rotation rule

Turn k (Quaternion.Euler(0, 90k, 0), clockwise from above): side sockets move posX -> negZ -> negX -> posZ -> posX unchanged. Top/bottom sockets with 4 or 5 parts move their last side part to the front (a middle part stays last); 1-part faces are unchanged.

Side profiles

Sides are read clockwise as seen from above (posX from +Z to −Z, negZ from +X to −X, …), so they are plotted as seen from inside the tile: u left to right, v up. Facing neighbours read the shared edge in opposite directions, so string reversal is the inverse. Symmetric profiles get palindromes; an asymmetric profile and its mirror get reversed strings. | is not allowed in side IDs.

Top and bottom

Plotted in Unity world (x, z) from above. With Top / bottom on, profiles equal under 90° rotation share a base letter: a face that looks the same in every rotation is one part (A), otherwise four parts like B0|B1|B2|B3 that turn with the tile. With it off, no top/bottom is written and Unity infers posX|negZ|negX|posZ from the sides.

Surface orientation

Points with a surface lying in a face plane also record which way it faces (from the triangle winding): out of the cell or into it. A roof on a tile's top and a ground on the bottom of the tile above share vertices but face the same way, so they would be two surfaces in one place; they get different sockets. Back-to-back surfaces (roof under a ceiling) match. Surfaces meeting a face edge-on (a wall ending at a side or crossing the top) record which way their front points, so a wall only continues or stacks on a wall facing the same way, like the handmade io/oi sockets.

Inside and outside

Each face is also sampled on a 4×4 grid: a sample is outside when the nearest surface seen from it shows its front, inside when it shows its back (inside wins a tie, nothing in reach counts as outside). Where a surface lies in the face, the sample takes the space beyond it, so a roof's top is outside. These labels are part of the socket, like the handmade i and o, so outside space only continues outside space. "Empty tiles" adds both an outside and an inside air tile (both -1), so building interiors can be filled.

A face whose only surface faces out of the cell (a roof on the top face) is open: the tile is already closed there, so it gets the empty face's socket and air can sit next to it. Open sockets are dotted-underlined in the tile list. A face that also has geometry crossing it (a wall carrying on upward) stays closed.

Reading the profile plots

Lines are mesh edges in the face plane. Squares mark surfaces lying in the face: filled ■ faces out of the cell, hollow □ faces into it. Dotted ticks show which way the front of a surface meeting the face edge-on (a wall) points. Shaded areas are inside (behind a surface), unshaded outside.

Axes

All face names use Unity axes after the axis mapping. Check the coloured axis labels in the tile inspector (bottom right) against Unity's scene view.

Help & Unity rules

Matching

Two tiles may be neighbours when the socket on one side equals the reversed socket on the facing side (ewf ↔ fwe, f ↔ f).

Unity rotation rule

Rotating a prototype k quarter turns clockwise viewed from above: horizontal sockets move posX -> negZ -> negX -> posZ -> posX (k steps). For posY and negY of the form XrX, set r = (r + k) % period[X]. Single-character vertical sockets are unchanged.

One clockwise quarter turn (viewed from above, +X toward −Z) is Quaternion.Euler(0, 90, 0) in Unity.

Horizontal profiles

Points are plotted as seen from outside the tile looking at the face: u left to right, v up. Facing neighbours are then mirror images, so string reversal is the inverse. Symmetric profiles get palindromes; an asymmetric profile and its mirror get reversed strings.

Vertical profiles

Top and bottom are plotted in Unity world (x, z) from above. Profiles equal under 90° rotation share a base letter; r counts clockwise quarter turns from the canonical form. Period 1 sockets are a single letter.

Axes

All face names use Unity axes after the axis mapping. Check the coloured axis labels in the preview against Unity's scene view.
